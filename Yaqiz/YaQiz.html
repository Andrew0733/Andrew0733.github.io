About Project

Your guardian on every road.

YAQIZ Driver Safety App

YAQIZ is an innovative mobile application designed to enhance driver and passenger safety through advanced monitoring technologies. Integrated with a state-of-the-art Driver Monitoring System, YAQIZ provides real-time insights into driver behavior, ensuring a safer driving experience.

The app combines facial recognition, biometric sensing, and smart alerting systems to detect fatigue, monitor stress, and respond to emergencies — before they happen.

🔐

Face ID Auth

Ensure only authorized drivers can access the vehicle, adding an extra layer of security.

💓

Heart Rate Monitoring

Continuously track the driver's heart rate to detect fatigue, stress, or potential health risks.

🚨

Emergency Contacts

Easily manage emergency contacts with instant alerts sent in case of an emergency.

Key Insights

What the data told us.

01

Fatigue Is the #1 Risk

94%Believe fatigue is a real danger
44%Personally experienced it while driving
90%Have no tool to detect it

Nearly all drivers recognise the risk — yet almost none have a solution. This gap directly shaped YAQIZ's core fatigue-detection and real-time alert system.

02

The Market Is Wide Open

42%Unaware any solution exists
10%Currently use a monitoring tool
14%Tried one — it didn't meet their needs

YAQIZ doesn't just solve a problem — it introduces an entire category to a massively underserved audience.

03

Alerts Over Dashboards

72%Top motivation: health on long drives
40%Want real-time alerts above all else
34%Cite focus loss & sleep as top risk

Drivers want instant, actionable warnings — not complex charts. This informed YAQIZ's minimal, alert-first UI philosophy.
