About This Work

Ads that stop the scroll.

In a world where users scroll past hundreds of posts every day, the only ads that matter are the ones that make them pause. These designs are built for exactly that — bold visuals, clear messaging, and irresistible calls to action.

From health-tech app launches to restaurant promotions and luxury product campaigns — each ad is tailored to its platform, audience, and brand identity. Designed in Photoshop and Illustrator, optimized for engagement.

📱

Platform-Native

Every ad is designed for its specific platform — Facebook, Instagram, or Stories — with correct dimensions and best practices.

🎯

Conversion-Focused

Bold headlines, clear CTAs, and visual hierarchy that guides the eye to action — not just pretty, but effective.

🎨

Brand-Aligned

Each design respects the client's brand identity — colors, typography, and tone stay consistent across every touchpoint.

Design Process

From brief to scroll-stopper.

Every ad follows a proven creative process — understanding the brand, studying the audience, crafting the visual, and refining every detail until it's impossible to ignore.

01

Brand & Brief

Understanding the brand identity, target audience, campaign goals, and platform requirements before any design begins.

02

Visual Concept

Exploring layout options, color palettes, and visual directions that align with the brand and grab attention instantly.

03

Craft & Compose

Building the final composition in Photoshop — product photography, typography, effects, and visual hierarchy working together.

04

Refine & Deliver

Polishing every pixel, testing readability at small sizes, and exporting in platform-optimized formats ready for launch.

LightStep — Health App Launch

Healthcare App Promotion Facebook

A bold Facebook ad campaign for LightStep — a health monitoring and doctor consultation app. The design showcases dual phone screens displaying the app's key features: health tracking dashboard with weekly reports, and a comprehensive doctor directory. The deep blue medical theme with heartbeat line accents reinforces trust and professionalism, while the "Your Health, One Tap Away" tagline creates urgency. App Store and Google Play badges drive immediate downloads.

Client LightStep Health

Hibiscus — Luxury Beverage Promo

Food & Beverage Product Launch Instagram

A rich, moody Instagram ad for a special hot hibiscus drink — designed to feel premium and irresistible. The dark crimson background with steam rising from a glass teacup creates an atmosphere of warmth and luxury. Gold and silver rose accents add an editorial, high-end feel that stands out in any feed. The "Save Up To 50% Off" badge drives urgency while the "Order Now" CTA converts browsers into buyers. Every element works together to make this drink feel like an experience, not just a product.

03

Pizzza — Restaurant Deal Campaign

Food & Dining Promotional Facebook

A mouth-watering Facebook ad for an Italian pizza restaurant — designed to make scrollers hungry on sight. The vibrant red and orange color scheme screams energy and appetite appeal. The hero composition features three beautifully photographed pizzas with a bold "Order 2 Large Pizza and Get a Medium Margherita For Free" offer that's impossible to ignore. The rustic wood texture at the bottom adds an authentic Italian feel, while the "More from Rome" tagline establishes brand origin. This ad doesn't just promote a deal — it makes you taste it.
