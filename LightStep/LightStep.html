About Project

Care that walks beside you.

Our application is designed to provide comprehensive support for individuals managing medical conditions like muscular atrophy. With a user-friendly interface, it allows users to track their symptoms, monitor progression, and receive personalised care plans.

The app integrates seamlessly with wearable devices to collect real-time health data, enabling accurate and timely insights for both patients and their care teams.

Brand Identity

A commitment to rehabilitation, mobility, and compassionate care.

LightStep's central figure — supported by an overhead rehabilitation system — symbolises individuals on their journey toward recovery and renewed independence. The caregiver standing beside them reflects guidance, encouragement, and human connection throughout the healing process.
